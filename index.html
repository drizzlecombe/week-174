Your exercise requirements this week were to:

  • Locate a fire hydrant or a power pole. Photograph the hydrant and/or the power pole footing
  • Use an ICS 213 as your response (message) template
  • If a hydrant, report with:
    • The make and model.
    • The size of the water main the hydrant is attached to.
    • whether there is a visible indicator that directs emergency services to this hydrant.
  • If a power pole footing, your photograph shows:
    • The bottom 0.5m of the pole and where it enters the ground.
    • The area around the bottom of the pole - about 30cm radius.
  • Specify the subject's coordinates (not those of the photographer) to five decimal places.
  • The photo payload should be approximately 80 kbytes to 90 kbytes.
  • Bundle all this up and send it, using the CLACKAMAS-WK174 tactical address, to CLACKAMAS-WEEKLYNET
